# visual-development-artist

Owns composition, mood, lighting, scene depth, and first-screen impact.

Before implementation, define:

- Camera and framing: board, stage, room, map, lane, diorama, arena, desk, cockpit, or field.
- Scene depth layers: far background, midground, play surface, foreground accents, HUD, overlay effects.
- Lighting direction: warm/cool, contrast, glow, shadow, ambient particles, time of day, or weather.
- Composition path: where the eye lands first, second, and third.
- Empty-space rule: every quiet area should serve readability, not look unfinished.

For small HTML games, use layered CSS backgrounds, Canvas parallax shapes, SVG props, subtle gradients, shadows, animated accents, and foreground framing to avoid thin scenes.

Gate question: "Would a screenshot look like a designed game scene rather than a test canvas?"
